# Sfurti Content

Sfurti creates opportunities for children to engage actively, apply meaningful effort, and develop capability while preserving curiosity and agency. Its content work is guided by the same mission.

## Language

**Meaningful effort**:
A child's active participation in making, experimenting, practicing, reasoning, solving, creating, or sustained exploration. Difficulty serves engagement and growth rather than being an end in itself.

**Child agency**:
A child's meaningful freedom to choose and act within an inviting, supportive experience.

**Passive consumption**:
Receiving stimulation without meaningful participation in deciding, creating, experimenting, solving, or practicing. This can occur in physical or digital experiences.

**On-mission experience**:
An experience that encourages meaningful effort and active engagement while respecting child agency and Sfurti's hard stops.

**Source video**:
An existing online video considered as source material for Sfurti content. Discovery alone does not establish suitability or permission to reuse it.

**Clip**:
A video excerpt or edited video derived from source material for Sfurti's Facebook page.

**Source segment**:
A time-bounded part of a source video used in a clip. Its start and end refer to positions in the original source, allowing the excerpt to be identified for future editing and reuse checks.

**Bangla-first discovery**:
A preference for original Bangla source videos when creating reused clips. Research discovery may use any language or region; parent-facing delivery remains Bangla.

**Custom request**:
A one-off content request that adds work without changing Sfurti's scheduled work or its default topic.

**Age-specific content**:
Content intended for parents of children within a stated part of Sfurti's 3–15 age range.

**General content**:
Content relevant across Sfurti's age range, with qualifications wherever an example or claim applies only to particular ages.

**Approved clip**:
A clip accepted by Sfurti's independent review after meeting its content and quality requirements. Approval makes its source segments used; it does not mean the clip has been published on Facebook.

**Pending source**:
A discovered source that appears suitable but awaits documented permission and an authorized source file before it can be edited.

**Daily content package**:
The scheduled default content for one Bangladesh calendar day. Its composition follows the selected publishing policy; additional custom requests do not satisfy its requirement.

**Mission image**:
An image post that communicates Sfurti's goal of meaningful effort, curiosity, and child agency.

**Scheduled post**:
An approved piece of content assigned a future Facebook publication time. Scheduling does not establish that publication has occurred.

**Content reserve**:
Approved, unpublished content available to fill a future daily package. Reserved content must still meet that package's topic, age, and source-diversity requirements.

**Posting window**:
A saved time range from which a future publication time can be selected. Windows are informed by one-time research and do not imply proven optimal engagement for Sfurti's audience.

**Content library**:
Stored generated content and its associated metadata, including review and publication status. The publishing scheduler selects eligible approved content from this library; file presence alone does not imply approval.

**Upload queue**:
Approved content selected for future Facebook publication, with its intended time and delivery state. Every queued item is represented in the queue CSV; queue membership does not establish successful remote scheduling or publication.

**Planning coverage**:
Future daily packages backed by approved content with assigned publication times and satisfying their configured counts and source-diversity requirements. Empty topic slots do not count toward coverage.

**Facebook-confirmed schedule**:
A future post whose scheduled state has been acknowledged by Facebook. It is preserved when future local plans are rebuilt after configuration changes.

## Research and evidence

The following terms describe the approved research-engine design; their presence does not imply implementation.

**Triage source**:
An internet resource considered for collecting parent questions, expert commentary or evidence, in any language or region. It is distinct from a source video and from an implementation ticket's triage status.

**Triage item**:
A collected, attributed observation or excerpt awaiting classification and topic validation. Collection does not establish factual reliability.

**Research topic**:
An answerable question with a defined population, activity or exposure, comparison and outcome where applicable.

**Finding**:
A source-specific result or recommendation with its original supporting passage, context and limitations.

**Evidence synthesis**:
An assessment of relevant findings for a research topic that preserves both agreement and disagreement.

**Claim version**:
A specific wording of a statement, its scope, evidence type and supporting findings. Revised wording or evidence creates a separately assessable version.

**Approved claim**:
A claim version that passed evidence review for its stated scope and remains valid. Approval does not establish that every paraphrase or application is supported.

**Qualified claim**:
An approved statement whose uncertainty, disagreement or applicability limits must accompany its use.

**Evidence packet**:
A selected set of current claim versions and original supporting passages supplied for a content or review task.

**Content brief**:
A distinct editorial angle and practical takeaway derived from a research topic and its approved claims.

**Original explainer**:
A Sfurti-created educational video using original narration and suitable visuals rather than requiring an excerpt from an existing source video.

**Audience signal**:
A reported experience, concern or question that helps identify parent needs. It is not scientific verification of its underlying assertions.

**Critical exception**:
A case requiring human attention because of consequential published misinformation, potentially harmful advice, privacy exposure, compromised integrity or unresolved containment of affected publication.

**Provisional reserve**:
Prepared future content that still requires evidence refresh or review before it can count toward verified planning coverage.
