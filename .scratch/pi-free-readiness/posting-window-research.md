# Provisional Bangladesh Facebook posting windows

Researched: 2026-09-15  
Scope: a Bangladesh-local Facebook Page serving Sfurti's parent audience. Times below are
`Asia/Dhaka` (UTC+06:00) local clock times.

## Finding

No accessible first-party Meta source establishes a universal "best" hour for a Bangladesh
Facebook audience. Meta's public Page-access guidance says that a person with appropriate
Facebook or task access can view Page, content and ad insights in Meta Business Suite, but it
does not publish Bangladesh hour-by-hour audience activity or an optimal-time rule.
[Facebook Help Centre: Page access](https://www.facebook.com/help/289207354498410/)

The one directly Bangladesh-specific, currently accessible timing source found was a local
commercial guide. It proposes an approximately 08:30--10:30 morning period, a 13:00--14:30
weekday lunch period, and an 20:00--22:30 Facebook evening period in BST.
[SMM Star Pro, "Best Time to Post for a Bangladesh Audience"](https://smmstarpro.com/best-time-to-post-for-bangladesh-audience/)

Treat those three ranges only as a **provisional starting hypothesis** for the initial
`posting-windows.json`, not as a claim of optimal engagement. At 60-minute operational
spacing they can provide at least six non-overlapping daily selections (for example 08:30,
09:30, 13:00, 14:00, 20:00 and 21:00), so they support the five-slot capacity check without
inventing additional hours. The 60-minute value is an anti-collision scheduling policy, not a
research finding about audience behaviour.

## Context that does *not* prove a time window

Meta advertising-resource data reported through DataReportal placed Facebook's Bangladesh ad
reach at 52.9 million in early 2024. The report expressly warns that estimated audience size is
not a proxy for daily/monthly active users or engagement. It supports Bangladesh relevance, not
any time-of-day inference.
[DataReportal, *Digital 2024: Bangladesh*](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-bangladesh#facebook-users-in-bangladesh-in-2024)

## Limitations and required refresh

- The timing guide is a commercial secondary source: it discloses no sample, Page population,
  collection period, method, outcome metric, or uncertainty. It is not evidence about Sfurti,
  parents, children, post format, reach, or engagement.
- The research found no public first-party Meta evidence for universal Bangladesh times. Meta
  help pages may also require an authenticated account, so the operator's actual Page view is
  the authoritative source when available.
- Do not call these windows optimal, current Page Insights, or a prediction of a Reel/photo/text
  result. Do not extrapolate a student, another country, or another platform's activity study to
  Sfurti parents.
- Before treating the schedule as learned rather than provisional, the operator should save the
  date, timezone, observed Meta Business Suite Active-times suggestions and Page/post metrics;
  compare like formats across slots over multiple posts; then replace this record/configuration
  with the dated Page-specific observation. Keep the source and these limitations with the
  replacement.
- If the local guide becomes unavailable, is materially changed, or the Page observation is
  absent and this weak source is deemed unacceptable, keep the posting-evidence readiness gate
  false rather than supplying invented evidence.
